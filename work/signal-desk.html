Outbound that starts from a reason to write, not from a list.

I built this at a crypto-compliance vendor, as part of the growth team. Compliance buyers don't answer cold lists. They answer when something just changed for them: a new license, a first compliance hire, a filing. So the work was to watch for those changes, rank them, and put a draft that names the change in front of a person within the hour.

02 · what I builtpython, node.js, scheduled jobs

Three systems, one rule: a person sends.

  1. Signal-based outbound.

    Monitors watch hiring signals, regulatory filings and public registries on a schedule. The strongest signals arrive hourly, each with a draft email that names the change, ready for review.

  2. Outbound infrastructure.

    Sending on Smartlead, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up on the sending domains. A Python pipeline validated and scored inbound event leads against the ICP, and that scored list fed a live campaign. A pre-send check catches unapproved claims and do-not-contact domains before anything goes out.

  3. A generated customer-facing page.

    The product's What's New page is a generated build from the product changelog, not a hand-edited page. It has a guarded edit tool and a rebuild test, so the public page can't drift from what actually shipped.

This is my employer's system, so the code, sources, scoring and data stay private and this page goes no deeper than my CV. The desk on the home page replays the shape on fictional companies. I can explain the design decisions in an interview without showing the code.
